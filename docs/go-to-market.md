# Go-to-Market Plan

## Positioning

Open Local Audit is a practical diagnostic tool for local business websites. It does not try to replace enterprise SEO suites. It finds the basics that prevent local businesses from converting search, map, Instagram, and referral traffic into calls or WhatsApp conversations.

## Primary audiences

1. TORUT internal operator.
2. Freelancers and small agencies.
3. Technical founders and indie operators.
4. Local business owners through TORUT-generated reports.

## Launch narrative

"We audited local business websites around Istanbul and found the same fixable issues: unclear contact actions, weak mobile pages, missing schema, outdated metadata, and broken trust signals. Open Local Audit makes those problems visible."

## Launch assets

- Public GitHub README.
- Example reports.
- Screenshots of Markdown reports.
- TORUT article explaining the first 10 checks.
- One demo page on `torut.dev`.

## Lead path

1. Run audit for a prospect.
2. Send short owner-readable report.
3. Offer TORUT Local Presence Kit if the business wants implementation.
4. Convert successful work into before/after case study.

## Initial content ideas

- "10 problems we keep finding on local business websites."
- "Why a WhatsApp link is not enough if your mobile page is confusing."
- "How small clinics and salons can improve local trust without a full SaaS system."
- "Open-source local website auditing for practical operators."

## Success metrics

- 20 real audits completed manually.
- 3 qualified Local Presence Kit conversations.
- 1 reference customer.
- 50 GitHub stars after public launch.
- 10 external users running the CLI.

## Positioning boundaries

Do not market as:
- a Google Maps scraper,
- an automated lead machine,
- a full SEO platform,
- a replacement for professional legal/privacy review.

Market as:
- a local website quality auditor,
- a practical evidence generator,
- an open-source trust asset from TORUT.
